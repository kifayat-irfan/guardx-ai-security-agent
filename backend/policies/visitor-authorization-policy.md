## Purpose

Ensure every visitor is identified, authorized, and escorted while on site,
and define how unescorted or unregistered visitors are handled.

## Rules

1. All visitors must register at reception with a government-issued photo ID
   and state the employee they are visiting.
2. The hosting employee must confirm the visit in the visitor log before the
   visitor proceeds beyond reception.
3. Visitors receive a visible visitor badge that must be worn at all times.
4. Visitors must be escorted by their host or a delegate at all times outside
   public waiting areas.
5. Visitor access is limited to the areas required for the visit purpose.
6. Photography and video recording by visitors is prohibited without written
   approval from the facility security officer.

## Severity Guidance

- An unescorted visitor in a non-public area is a MEDIUM severity event.
- An unescorted visitor inside a restricted zone is a HIGH severity event.
- A visitor without a visible badge in office areas is a LOW severity event
  (verify and re-badge).

## Recommended Response

1. Approach the visitor, verify identity and badge, and locate their host.
2. If no host can be identified, escort the visitor to reception for
   re-registration.
3. If the visitor entered a restricted zone unescorted, treat as a restricted
   area breach and follow that policy's response steps.
4. Record the encounter in the visitor log with camera references.
