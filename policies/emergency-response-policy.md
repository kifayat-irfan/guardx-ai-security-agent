## Purpose

Define how GuardX detections feed into emergency situations (fire, medical
emergency, security threat) and who takes charge when one is declared.

## Rules

1. Any person may trigger an emergency alert via wall-mounted panic buttons
   or by calling the emergency extension.
2. On a confirmed emergency, restricted-zone access rules are suspended for
   emergency responders and evacuation routes.
3. Elevators must not be used during a fire alarm; stairwells are the
   designated evacuation paths.
4. The incident commander (facility security officer, or senior manager on
   duty) has final authority during an emergency.
5. All-clear may only be declared by the incident commander after responders
   confirm the area is safe.

## Severity Guidance

- Confirmed fire, medical emergency, or active threat is CRITICAL severity.
- A panic-button press that cannot be immediately verified is HIGH severity
  until verified.
- Drill or test activations announced in advance are LOW severity.

## Recommended Response

1. Confirm the emergency via a second source (second camera, sensor, or
   human report) before full escalation.
2. Alert emergency services immediately for CRITICAL events.
3. Initiate evacuation announcements for the affected zones.
4. Preserve all camera footage from 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after
   the event for investigation.
