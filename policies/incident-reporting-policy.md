## Purpose

Ensure every security incident is documented consistently, with the evidence
needed for review, insurance, and legal purposes.

## Rules

1. Every HIGH or CRITICAL severity event must have an incident report filed
   within 24 hours.
2. The report must include: date/time, camera ID, zone name, a description
   of what happened, the people involved (if identified), actions taken,
   and the outcome.
3. Video evidence must be exported and attached: at least 2 minutes before
   the first detection and 2 minutes after the last.
4. Reports are reviewed by the facility security officer within 48 hours.
5. Incident data is retained for a minimum of 2 years and access is limited
   to security staff and management.

## Severity Guidance

- Missing or late reports for HIGH/CRITICAL events are themselves a MEDIUM
  severity compliance issue.
- Reports must never include speculation about motives; record observable
  facts only.

## Recommended Response

1. Generate the draft report from the GuardX incident record as soon as the
   situation is resolved.
2. Attach the required video evidence and policy citations used in the
   assessment.
3. Submit for security-officer review and track to closure.
