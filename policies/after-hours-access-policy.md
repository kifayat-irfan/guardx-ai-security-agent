## Purpose

Control who may be present in the facility outside normal operating hours
(18:00–08:00 local time, weekends, and public holidays) and define how
after-hours presence is monitored.

## Rules

1. Normal operating hours are 08:00–18:00 local time, Monday to Friday.
2. After-hours presence requires a pre-approved after-hours authorization
   logged before 17:00 on the same day.
3. Cleaning and maintenance crews may only enter during their scheduled
   windows, published weekly by facilities management.
4. All after-hours entrants must badge in at the main entrance; badge records
   are the authoritative attendance log.
5. Motion detected in office areas after hours without a matching badge record
   is treated as a potential intrusion.

## Severity Guidance

- Unbadged presence after hours in any area is a HIGH severity event.
- Badged presence after hours outside the authorized person's approved zones
  is a MEDIUM severity event.
- Scheduled cleaning crew activity within their window is LOW severity.

## Recommended Response

1. Cross-check the detected person against the after-hours authorization list
   and badge records.
2. If no authorization exists, dispatch security to verify identity on site.
3. If the person cannot produce authorization, escort them out and file an
   incident report.
4. Notify the person's department manager on the next business day.
